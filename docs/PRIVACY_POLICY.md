## 中文版 (Chinese Version)

### 一、 概述与“本地优先”核心准则
感谢您使用 GoTime（以下简称“本应用”或“我们”）。我们高度重视您的个人隐私与数据安全。  
本应用基于 **“本地优先 (Local-First)”** 与 **“极简纯净 (Privacy-by-Design)”** 理念构建：
1. **零第三方广告**：本应用不包含任何第三方商业广告 SDK。
2. **零用户画像与数据买卖**：我们绝不收集、出售、转让或共享任何用户的个人敏感数据给任何第三方数据代理商或广告平台。
3. **本地离线自治**：您的习惯名称、打卡记录、专注时长、心得日志均默认仅加密存储在您的本地设备数据库中（SQLite）。

---

### 二、 应用申请的权限及数据使用说明 (Google Play / Apple 合规披露)

为了向您提供核心习惯追踪与专注服务，本应用可能会在必要时向您申请以下权限，每项权限均受严格的最小必要原则约束：

#### 1. 系统健康数据与运动传感器 (`Health Connect` / `Apple Health` / `ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION`)
- **使用目的**：仅用于“自动感应打卡”功能（例如当用户在健康应用中的步数达到目标，或记录到睡眠周期时，本地自动匹配并完成对应的计数习惯）。
- **严格承诺 (遵从 Google Play Health Connect 政策)**：
  - 本应用仅在获得您明确授权后读取指定的健康指标（如步数、睡眠时长）。
  - **绝不上传健康数据至任何外部服务器**，所有比对与打卡计算均在本地设备运行。
  - **绝不将健康数据用于广告投放、信用评估、保险定价或任何商业变现目的**。

#### 2. 系统通知与闹钟提醒权限 (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS` / `SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM`)
- **使用目的**：用于按照您自定义的提醒时间（如晨间 08:00、睡前 21:00）发送本地提醒通知，帮助您维持习惯动量。
- **数据范围**：通知完全由本地定时器调度触发，不经过外部推送中继服务器。

#### 3. 生物识别与硬件安全锁 (`USE_BIOMETRIC` / `USE_FINGERPRINT`)
- **使用目的**：用于“应用隐私安全锁”功能（支持 Face ID、指纹识别及 4 位数字 PIN 码），保护您的打卡记录不被他人窥视。
- **数据范围**：生物识别验证完全由操作系统底层（Android Keymaster / Apple Secure Enclave）执行，本应用无法获取也绝不存储您的指纹或面部生物特征数据。

#### 4. 本地存储与文件读写 (`Scoped Storage` / 文件选择器)
- **使用目的**：用于全量数据的本地 JSON/CSV 格式备份、导出与恢复，以及 WebDAV 私有网盘同步文件的临时处理。
- **数据范围**：仅访问您主动选择保存或导入的备份文件，绝不扫描或访问您设备中的其他相册或私密文件。

#### 5. 网络访问权限 (`INTERNET` / `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`)
- **使用目的**：仅在您主动配置并触发 **WebDAV 私有云同步**（如坚果云、Nextcloud）或 **设备间增量合库** 时连接您指定的网盘服务器。
- **安全保障**：所有网络传输强制使用 TLS/HTTPS 加密协议，WebDAV 鉴权凭证仅在本地安全加密存储。

### 四、 用户权利：数据导出与物理粉碎 (GDPR / CCPA / 审核死线)
根据《通用数据保护条例》(GDPR)、《加利福尼亚州消费者隐私法》(CCPA) 及苹果与谷歌应用商店审核规范，您享有以下绝对权利：
1. **完整导出权**：您可随时在【设置】->【本地全量数据备份】中导出全部习惯与打卡数据为 JSON 或 CSV 开放格式。
2. **账号注销与数据粉碎权**：您可在【设置】最底部随时点击“注销账号并粉碎所有数据”，系统将立即连带物理粉碎本地 SQLite 数据库中的所有记录，不留存任何痕迹与残留数据。

## English Version (Google Play & App Store Compliance)

### 1. Overview & Local-First Philosophy
Thank you for using GoTime ("the App", "we", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and personal data.  
GoTime is engineered upon a **Local-First** and **Privacy-by-Design** architecture:
- **No Third-Party Ads**: The App contains zero commercial advertisement SDKs.
- **No Data Selling or Profiling**: We never collect, sell, lease, or distribute your personal or sensitive data to third-party data brokers or marketing platforms.
- **Local Autonomy**: Your habits, check-in history, focus logs, and reflections are stored exclusively in your local SQLite database on your device by default.

---

### 2. Permissions Requested & Data Usage

To deliver essential habit tracking and focus timer functionality, the App may request the following system permissions under strict necessity:

#### 2.1 Health & Fitness Data (`Health Connect` / `Apple Health` / `ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION`)
- **Purpose**: Exclusively utilized for the optional "Sensory Auto Check-in" feature (e.g., automatically completing a counter habit when your daily step count reaches its goal or sleep duration is logged).
- **Google Play Health Connect Policy Compliance**:
  - The App reads health indicators solely upon your explicit, opt-in permission.
  - **Health data is processed 100% locally on your device and is NEVER transferred or uploaded to external servers**.
  - **Health data is NEVER used for advertising, credit scoring, underwriting, or any commercial monetization purposes**.

#### 2.2 Notifications & Alarms (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS` / `SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM`)
- **Purpose**: Used exclusively to trigger scheduled reminders locally on your device at your designated times.
- **Scope**: Reminders are strictly generated by local operating system alarm schedulers without remote push relays.

#### 2.3 Biometrics & Device Security (`USE_BIOMETRIC` / `USE_FINGERPRINT`)
- **Purpose**: Powers the optional "App Privacy Lock" (Face ID / Fingerprint / 4-Digit PIN) to shield your data from unauthorized physical access.
- **Scope**: Biometric verification is handled entirely by your device hardware (Android Keymaster / Apple Secure Enclave); the App never captures or stores biometric data.

#### 2.4 File Storage & Backups (`Scoped Storage` / File Picker)
- **Purpose**: Enables exporting and restoring local JSON/CSV full backups and processing WebDAV cloud backup archives.
- **Scope**: Access is restricted strictly to files you explicitly pick or export.

#### 2.5 Internet Access (`INTERNET` / `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`)
- **Purpose**: Active only when you initiate synchronization with your private WebDAV cloud storage (e.g., Nextcloud, Nutstore) or peer-to-peer cloud sync.
- **Security**: All network transmissions enforce TLS/HTTPS encryption. Credentials are stored securely on the local device.

---

### 3. Zero Third-Party Analytics or Tracking SDKs
GoTime does not embed any telemetry, crash beacons, or behavioral tracking SDKs (e.g., Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, or third-party ad networks). Your usage patterns and habit streaks remain confidential on your device.

---

### 4. User Rights: Data Portability & Complete Erasure (GDPR / CCPA)
Under GDPR, CCPA, and application marketplace policies, you retain full ownership of your data:
1. **Data Portability**: Export your complete database at any time in standard JSON or CSV formats.
2. **Right to Erasure (Account Deletion & Data Shredding)**: Use the "Delete Account & Shred All Data" button in Settings to permanently and irreversibly wipe your local database and all associated records.

---

### 5. Children's Privacy (COPPA)
GoTime does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13 (or applicable local age threshold). Because data resides locally without mandatory account registration, children's privacy is preserved by default.
